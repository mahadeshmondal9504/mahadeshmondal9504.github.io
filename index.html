About Me

Interdisciplinary chemistry researcher with a background in Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering at the University of Dhaka, having a clear vision to contribute to the fields of pharmaceutical chemistry, drug delivery, nanomedicine, computational chemistry, and advanced functional materials.

My research experience includes studying drug loading and release mechanisms for sustained drug delivery, protein- and biopolymer-based drug carriers, functionalized biomaterials for targeted therapeutics, nanoparticles, and nanocomposites. This interdisciplinary experience has further motivated my interest in molecular modeling and computational & data-driven research, particularly in the design of functionalized flavonoid derivatives for cancer therapeutics.

I also have research experience in advanced functional materials and environmental applications, including graphene-based nanocomposites, alloys, and nanomaterials for applications in oil recovery, photocatalysis, and biomedicals. I am particularly interested in integrating medicinal chemistry, organic synthesis, and nanotechnology with computational chemistry and molecular modeling to develop innovative drug delivery platforms and therapeutic strategies. My long-term research goal is to bridge experimental and computational approaches in drug discovery, pharmaceutical development, and nanomedicine.
