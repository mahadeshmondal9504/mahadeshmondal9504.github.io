About Me

I am a chemistry researcher working at the intersection of computational chemistry and machine learning. My interests span QSAR/QSPR modeling, molecular docking and dynamics, and data-driven approaches to materials and drug-related problems — including graphene-based nanomaterials.

I build reproducible, open-source workflows: from data curation and descriptor calculation to model training, validation, and publication-ready figures.

Research Interests

Computational Chemistry

Molecular modeling, docking, and simulation to understand structure–property relationships.

Machine & Deep Learning

Predictive modeling on chemical data — regression, classification, and neural networks.

QSAR / QSPR Modeling

Descriptor-based models linking molecular structure to activity, toxicity, or material properties.

Nanomaterials

Graphene-based and composite materials for environmental and biomedical applications.
